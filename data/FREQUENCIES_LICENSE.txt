word_frequencies_en.tsv
=======================

What it is: English word frequencies (occurrences per billion words) for
the lowercase forms of the words in words.txt, taken from the "large"
English list of wordfreq 3.1.1 and rounded to whole numbers. Built by
tools/build_frequencies.py.

Source: wordfreq by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
Citation: R. Speer, rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0, Zenodo, 2022,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.7199437
