word_frequencies_en.tsv
=======================

What it is: English word frequencies (occurrences per billion words) from
the "large" English list of wordfreq 3.1.1, rounded to whole numbers: the
lowercase forms of the words in words.txt plus every other wordfreq word
made of the letters a-z (the app adds those to its vocabulary). Built by
tools/build_frequencies.py.

Source: wordfreq by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
Citation: R. Speer, rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0, Zenodo, 2022,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.7199437
